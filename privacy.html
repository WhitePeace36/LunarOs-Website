Privacy policy

Information under Art. 13 GDPR · Last updated: November 2026

The short version
  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No third-party requests when you open a page. Fonts and images come from this server.
  • The web server keeps standard access logs for 2 months, then deletes them.

1. Overview

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website, why, and what rights you have. It applies to this website only, not to the LunarOs operating system itself.

3. Data collected when you visit

Every time a page or file is requested, the server automatically records the following in its access logs:

  • IP address of the requesting device, stored only in shortened form: Hetzner replaces the last part of IPv4 addresses with a random value and anonymises the last 88 bits of IPv6 addresses, so the address can no longer be traced to you
  • date and time of the request
  • requested page or file and the amount of data transferred
  • referrer URL (the page you came from)
  • browser type and version, and operating system

Purpose: delivering the website and keeping it stable and secure, for example by detecting attacks.
Legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
Retention: log files are deleted after 2 months, unless a specific security incident requires keeping them longer.

4. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and embeds no third-party content. Fonts are served from this server, so no data is sent to Google Fonts or any other provider when you open a page. No cookie banner is needed for that reason.

5. Downloads and external links

The installation image (ISO) is downloaded directly from this website. A download is recorded in the server's access logs like any other request (see section 3); no other data is collected. Links to GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) lead to an external website: no data is sent to GitHub until you click such a link, and GitHub's own privacy policy applies there.

6. Contacting me

If you email me, I use your email address and the content of your message only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

7. Recipients

This website is hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, which processes the access logs on my behalf under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Personal data is not sold or passed on to anyone else, unless required by law.

8. Transfers outside the EU

The website is hosted in Hetzner data centres in Germany. No data is transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data held about you (Art. 15)
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)

To use these rights, email the address under Controller.

If you believe your data is being processed unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria, www.dsb.gv.at, or with the supervisory authority in your own EU country.

10. Data security

This website is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Access to the server and its logs is restricted to the operator and the hosting provider.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy is updated when the website or the way it processes data changes, for example if the download moves to another host. The date at the top shows the latest version.
